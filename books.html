Books

Global Land-Ocean Geospatial Applications

Mapping and Surveying with Remote Sensing and GIS. Edited with Biswajeet Pradhan. CRC Press / Taylor & Francis, 2025.

A survey of where geospatial analysis is actually going, spanning land and marine applications. I edited the volume and wrote five of its chapters — roughly a hundred of its pages, including the opening framing chapter, the closing synthesis, and a sole-authored chapter on energy:

  • “Introduction to Geospatial Analysis and Applications,” with M. T. Hassan and L. Miralha (pp. 1–15).
  • “Innovations in Ocean and Marine Spatial Planning,” with G. Milner and E. Ntsiful (pp. 138–154).
  • “Participatory Mapping Using GIS and RS across Regions,” with R. Kemper, A. Ryan and D. Oliveira (pp. 155–187).
  • “Energy Optimization and Innovation in GIS and Remote Sensing” — sole author (pp. 188–215).
  • “Toward a Spatial Future: The Global Promise of GIS and Remote Sensing,” with B. Pradhan (pp. 234–244).

Evaluating Participatory Mapping Software

Editor and contributing author. Springer, 2023.

A comparative evaluation of participatory mapping platforms, judged against what the communities and practitioners using them actually need rather than what the feature lists claim. My most-cited book-length work, and the basis for the PPGIS Evaluation plug-in. I wrote the volume’s opening and closing chapters:

  • “Participatory mapping and technology,” with M. McCall and A. D. Ollivierre (pp. 1–20) — the framing chapter setting out the evaluation problem.
  • “Trends, conclusions and recommendations for the future of participatory mapping software,” with P. A. Kwaku Kyem (pp. 235–257) — the closing synthesis.

Supporting Shrinkage

Better Planning and Decision-Making for Legacy Cities. With Michael P. Johnson Jr., Justin B. Hollander, Eliza W. Kinsey and George R. Chichirau. SUNY Press, 2021.

Citizen-engaged planning methods for cities and regions facing decline, disinvestment and shrinkage, built around case studies of three US cities and the analytic approaches — collaborative planning, civic technology, decision modeling, spatial analysis — available to them.

Chapters in other editors’ books

  • “How Intersectionality-Based Approaches to International Development Illuminate the Plight of Palestine Refugees,” in The Palgrave Handbook of Intersectionality in Public Policy, ed. Hankivsky & Jordan-Zachery. Palgrave Macmillan, 2019.
  • “Participatory mapping,” with Ollivierre and Lucchesi, in International Encyclopedia of Geography. Wiley, 2016.
