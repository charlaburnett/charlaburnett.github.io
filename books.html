Books

Global Land-Ocean Geospatial Applications

Mapping and Surveying with Remote Sensing and GIS. Edited with Biswajeet Pradhan. CRC Press / Taylor & Francis, 2025.

A survey of where geospatial analysis is actually going, spanning land and marine applications: participatory mapping across regions, ocean and marine spatial planning, energy optimization, and the methodological foundations underneath all of it. I contributed the introduction, the closing chapter on the future of GIS and remote sensing, and chapters on energy optimization and on marine spatial planning.

Evaluating Participatory Mapping Software

Edited volume. Springer, 2023.

A comparative evaluation of participatory mapping platforms, judged against what the communities and practitioners using them actually need rather than what the feature lists claim. My most-cited book-length work, and the basis for the PPGIS Evaluation plug-in.

Supporting Shrinkage

Better Planning and Decision-Making for Legacy Cities. With Michael P. Johnson Jr., Justin B. Hollander, Eliza W. Kinsey and George R. Chichirau. SUNY Press, 2021.

Citizen-engaged planning methods for cities and regions facing decline, disinvestment and shrinkage, built around case studies of three US cities and the analytic approaches — collaborative planning, civic technology, decision modeling, spatial analysis — available to them.
